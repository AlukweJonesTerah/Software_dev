Keeper rituals

Practices for tending the nocturnal archive.

A set of small customs for those who keep the seeds and their stories. These rituals help the archive stay luminous, responsive, and ready for visitors.

Evening mist

Mist the trays with faint rainwater at dusk to keep the seeds cool and encourage their glow.

Quiet reading

Read a single note aloud beneath the specimen light before you close the room for the night.

Moon soil ritual

Dip your fingertips in moon soil and trace a circle around the tray to renew the archive's compass.
