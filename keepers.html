Seed keepers

Stories from the hands that tend the archive.

Meet the caretakers who watch over the nocturnal specimens. Their notes describe family traditions, midnight habits, and the subtle cues they use to know the room is ready.

Mirelle

She checks each tray at twilight and hums a low tune to coax the seeds into a softer glow.

Jun

Jun records the scent memory of every specimen and keeps a small jar of dried petals for each new visitor.

Amaya

Amaya arranges the trays by moon phase and writes the archive’s tasks in silver ink each evening.
