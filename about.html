Archive story

Where moonlit seeds are collected and curated.

Nocturne Seed Bank is a quiet library for speculative botanicals that hum with after-dark magic. Each tray contains a story, a scent, and a color that belongs to an imagined weather cycle.

Origin

Gathered from field notes written during midnight walks, these specimens are meant to feel like a living catalog of nocturnal memory.

Mission

To make the strange feel familiar through lush descriptions and atmospheric imagery, and to bring a breath of night into every page.

Visit

Requests are invited at the archive’s visiting window. Visitors may choose a tray based on glow, scent, or the weather they want to remember.
